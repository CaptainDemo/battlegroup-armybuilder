# NORTHAG 2020 Soviet roster release

Catalog `russian-armoured-battlegroup-2020/1.0.0` uses NORTHAG 2020 and the applicable parts of Official Rules Errata v9 (9 May 2025). The catalog has 49 top-level purchases, 10 attachment entries, 47 options and 93 independent profile records. The source navigation and fingerprints are in [the source baseline](northag-baseline.md).

The NORTHAG workspace owns catalog/settings validation and all list checks. The catalog authoring files transcribe the list and arsenal separately from Classic. The Web adapter supplies section counts, training, selectable members, special-rule text and its own equipment appendix. Its armour uses Front/Side/Rear or Soft Skin. Weapon rows use five range bands and preserve non-applicable cells. The immutable catalog and its digest are generated by `node packages/catalogs/northag/author/build-soviet.mjs`. Only indexed public files are served.

| Source decision | Release behavior |
|---|---|
| Book pp.56, 69–74 | A Forward Screen choice is required and a defence or other non-model choice counts. Exactly one Vanguard is required. Same-name entries in different sections have separate limits. |
| Book pp.70–71 | Tank Company replacement prices are per company. Each tank has a stable slot and one may receive KMT. The company has no additional organization invented for its ten tanks. |
| NAG-01, book pp.70–71 and user decision | One or two optional weapon teams share one extra vehicle in their own platoon. The platoon has four vehicles while either team is present. The extra vehicle defaults to BTR-60 and has a free BTR-70 choice. It follows the fixed-price BMP-1 and eligible BMP-2 conversions with no extra Points or BR. |
| NAG-02, book pp.51–53, 69, errata PDF p.6 and user decision | Each command purchase with Officer or Senior Officer contributes one Officer. Each purchased Scout box contributes one Scout regardless of its number of vehicles or teams. An explicit Scout (2) or Scout (3) weight applies once to the box. |
| NAG-03, book pp.70–71 | The +16 BMP-2 follow-on upgrade applies to at most one purchased Main Column Motor Rifle Platoon and requires its +36 BMP-1 conversion. The Vanguard company has its separately printed +108 BMP-1 option. |
| NAG-04, printed example | The disputed 499 Points / 58 vs 66 BR example is not used as a golden roster. Independent test fragments reproduce 327 Points / 30 BR after two paid requests and all four TCC extras. |
| NAG-05, book pp.94–100, 106, HWC v6 PDF p.4 and user decision | The five K command tank variants use the corresponding NORTHAG base tank values in their own records. BTR-60 R and PU equipment comes from the visually checked community cards WAR-15a and WAR-15b, with v9 Amphibious applied. Captured Land Rover uses the book's Land Rover row. Ambulance Truck equipment remains unverified. |
| NAG-06, book pp.56–57, 74–75 | Restricted counts once per marked purchase or chosen upgrade. Mortar Spotter is a battlefield-use condition. Enablement grants one free request and extra requests cost 30 Points / 0 BR, with size-dependent caps of 3, 4 or 5 including the free one. Arrival options and their BR remain battle-time reference, outside the roster totals. |

The errata ledger records applied corrections against concrete entry/profile IDs. In particular, the p.69 Forward Screen observation officer drops the old upgrade, and the p.72 Tactical Command Centre can take up to one each of four different children. The Main Column observer retains its distinct vehicle options. The close-support gun is 2S1 Gvozdika and the p.71 carrier option is Konkurs. Timing, scenario resolution, reinforcement outcomes and combat procedures are outside static roster checks.
